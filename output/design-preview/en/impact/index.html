Method

Estimated avoided emissions = completed borrows × new-item footprint of the category reference × purchase-avoidance rate (25%).

The purchase-avoidance rate follows the user surveys of Library of Things (UK), which used 25% and updated it to 50% in 2024. We use the more conservative 25%. Once our own post-borrow survey has 30 yes/no answers, the rate switches automatically to its result (yes ÷ (yes + no)). Method source ↗

Each category is represented by one reference item with published data, deliberately using lower figures. Categories without reliable data are excluded from the total. Transport, packaging and disposal are not included. Only borrows where both parties confirmed the return are counted.
