Community rules

Run by Box Box for the CityUHK community. Not an official CityUHK platform or endorsed by the university.

01 · Identity & privacy

We verify access to an approved school mailbox, not enrolment. Do not share school passwords, identity documents or full email addresses. Private messages and order photos are restricted to participants and authorised administrators.

02 · Cancellation

Either party may cancel before handover, releasing the dates. Notify the other party early. No automatic cancellation fees. After handover, open a dispute and agree on a resolution with moderator assistance.

03 · Overdue loans & extensions

Return on time. Request extensions in advance; only lender approval makes them effective, subject to later bookings. Overdue hours use Hong Kong time and round up. No automatic charges; any extra fee needs explicit mutual agreement.

04 · Damage & condition

Inspect items at handover and return. Both parties should upload photos and confirm separately. Report loss or damage promptly and retain evidence. Do not seize another person’s belongings. Moderators can facilitate discussion but do not provide insurance, compensation or enforcement.

05 · Prohibited listings

No illegal or controlled goods, weapons, dangerous chemicals, medicines, tobacco or alcohol, pirated or stolen items, live animals, account credentials, student IDs or access cards. Privacy violations, fraud and harassment are prohibited. Moderators may remove content or restrict accounts.

06 · Rent, deposits & payment

Rent is in HKD per day, rounding any partial 24-hour period up. Rates, deposits and terms are saved when applying. Payments are offline. Confirmation records are self-reported and are not bank evidence. The platform does not collect funds, escrow deposits or make automatic deductions.

07 · Reports & disputes

Report items, users or orders through their report links. Reservations remain locked during disputes. Moderators review evidence and record outcomes. For immediate danger, contact local emergency services.

08 · Buddy & friends

Buddy is for finding people to study, play sports, eat or attend campus events with, or teammates for course projects, competitions, hackathons and startups. It is separate from borrowing. Spots do not count the host; the host accepts or declines each application, and nobody can join once a post is full or closed. Meet somewhere public on campus first and tell a friend your plans. A friendship needs the other person to accept, and people you trade or join activities with are never added automatically; removing a friend closes your chat. Harassment, impersonation, scams or inappropriate content lead to removal and account restriction; use the report links to tell the moderators.
